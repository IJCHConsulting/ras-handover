www.rasltd.co — findings from the Semrush project and a live crawl of the homepage, ahead of account transfer.

Domain rasltd.co Market United Kingdom Source Semrush · live crawl Prepared 2 Sep 2026
01

Summary

RAS already holds a strong foothold on its core term — #1 for "rope access solutions" — and carries a healthy 90 Authority Score. But the account is largely uninstrumented: Google Analytics/Search Console aren't feeding the Semrush project, only four keywords are being tracked against a site with nine-plus distinct service lines, and none of Semrush's audit tools (Site Audit, On-Page Checker, Backlink Audit) have been set up. In effect, the visible ranking data is a thin, accurate slice of a picture that's mostly dark. Before drawing conclusions about real performance, the first job is switching the lights on.

03

Findings log

P1‑01Immediate

No Google Analytics or Search Console feeding the project

Traffic Analytics, Organic Traffic, Organic Keywords and Paid Traffic are all reporting zero, and the site-wide Organic Rankings widget can't find the domain at all. This isn't a performance problem — it's a wiring problem. Nothing about actual site performance can be judged until this is connected.

P1‑02Immediate

Position Tracking covers 4 keywords against 9+ service lines

Rockfall mitigation, slope stabilisation, vegetation management, concrete & masonry repair, drainage remediation and drilling are all offered on-site but none are in the tracked keyword set. Real visibility across the business is currently unknown.

P1‑03Immediate

Site Audit, On-Page Checker and Backlink Audit are unconfigured

All three show "Set up" rather than results. There is no crawl-based technical audit, no content-gap analysis and no backlink toxicity check on record yet — these establish the actual baseline this document can only approximate from a single-page crawl.

P2‑01Scheduled

Meta description runs to 338 characters

Google typically displays roughly 155–160 characters before truncating. The current homepage description is more than double that, so the back half is never seen in search results — wasted copy that should be doing work.

P2‑02Scheduled

5 of 31 homepage images have no alt text

A gap for both accessibility and image search — Google Images is a plausible discovery channel for site/inspection photography that RAS likely has plenty of.

P2‑03Scheduled

og:image is served over http:// on an https:// page

A mixed-content link-preview image can silently fail to render when the page is shared on Slack, LinkedIn or iMessage. One-line fix in the site's social settings.

P3‑01Monitor

Heading capitalisation is inconsistent

Section headings mix Title Case, Sentence case and ALL CAPS ("Rockfall mitigation" vs. "CONCRETE & MASONRY REPAIRS"). Cosmetic, but worth a pass for brand consistency at the next content update.

P3‑02Monitor

90 Authority Score sits on a thin 24-domain base

Not necessarily a problem, but worth confirming with a proper Backlink Audit once it's set up (P1‑03) — a small referring-domain count carrying a high score is worth a quality check rather than taking at face value.

P3‑03Monitor

Zero AI-search visibility (ChatGPT, AI Overview, AI Mode, Gemini)

Not urgent relative to the gaps above, but worth a baseline note now that this figure sits at zero, ahead of tracking it as that referral channel grows.

04

Where the upside is

A genuine #1 to build around

"Rope access solutions" at position 1 is the anchor asset. It's worth deliberately building topical authority outward from it — internal links from service pages into the ranking page, and content that targets the adjacent terms searchers use next.

Scotland shows a regional opening

Two of the four tracked terms are Scotland-specific and sit at 16 and 32 — close enough to be winnable with dedicated location content, rather than starting cold.

Nine service lines, almost no dedicated tracking

Once keyword tracking is widened (P1‑02), there's a reasonable chance several service pages are already ranking somewhere in the top 100 without anyone knowing it — cheaper wins than starting from zero.

05

First 30 days

  • Connect Google Analytics + Search Console to the Semrush project — unblocks every zeroed-out metric on the dashboard.
  • Run Site Audit, On-Page Checker and Backlink Audit — turns this single-page crawl into a full technical baseline.
  • Expand Position Tracking to cover all core service terms, plus the Scotland-specific variants.
  • Trim the meta description to ~155 characters and rewrite og:image to https.
  • Add alt text to the 5 flagged homepage images.
